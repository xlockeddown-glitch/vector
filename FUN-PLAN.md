# VECTOR — simple plan

Two systems. That is the whole game.

1. Tower defense. Plant guns. Stop enemies. Keep Home alive.
2. Draft. See 3 cards. Pick 1.

Fun comes from **big cool cards** and **a fight you can watch**.

---

## What is wrong now

The cards are too small. They look like UI chips, not cards.

You sit in packs too long before the first shot.

Too many extra systems sit on top of those two: parts, pauses, meters, extra pack types.

---

## 1. Cards — this is the key

A card should look like a real trading card.

**Size**
- Playing-card shape: 2.5 by 3.5 (we already use 5:7).
- On a phone, a card you can pick must be **big**. Not a postage stamp in a grid.
- Pick screen: three fat cards. You can read the art without squinting.
- If three-across is too tight, fan them (one big in front, two behind) or swipe one-at-a-time. Do not shrink them to fit.

**Look**
- Art is most of the card. Gun or craft fills the window. Close. Moving a little (bob / spin) is good.
- Name in the VECTOR font, big.
- One short line of what it does. Example: “Punches through the line.”
- Gem shows rarity. Foil on the good ones. No extra stamps, chips, or field lists on the face.
- The card has weight: thick border, gem color, a little gloss. It should feel like you could pick it up.

**When you pick**
- The card you tap gets huge for a beat, then you plant it.
- That moment is the reward. Make it feel like opening a pack in a real card game.

---

## 2. Simple tower defense

Enemies walk the warp lane. You plant guns on moons. Crafts fly. If enemies reach Home, you lose a life. Lives hit zero, run over.

That is the loop. Do not add extra drama on top.

**A round**
1. Plant the guns you already picked.
2. Wave walks. You watch. Drag a craft onto a gun to help.
3. Wave ends.
4. Next wave. No pack. No pause.

Draft is **only at the start**. You build the defense, then you play it. More waves, same guns.

**Get to the fight**
- One craft already flying (your main).
- A short draft: a few packs of 3-pick-1. Guns and maybe one extra craft.
- Plant.
- Fight the whole run. No cards during waves. No cards between waves.

**Keep it readable**
- First wave is a few basic enemies. You learn the gun.
- Later waves add more, or tougher ones. Normal TD curve. No named “scare” events, no boss theater, no extra HUD warnings.

**Get to the fight fast**
- One craft already flying.
- Short draft at the start: a few 3-pick-1 packs (guns, maybe a craft).
- Plant. Fight. That is the rest of the run.

Do not freeze the fight to show a card. Do not draft between waves. Cards are how you **build**. The lane is how you **play**.

---

## 3. Simple draft

Only at the start of a run. That is how you build the defense.

- 3 cards.
- Pick 1.
- Same kind in the pack (3 guns, or 3 crafts).
- Same rarity so the pack is fair.

A few of these packs, then you plant, then you play. Not five packs plus more later.

No draft between waves. No draft mid-fight. No ban. The cards **are** the build screen.

After you plant, the rest of the game is tower defense. Watch the lane. Drag crafts. Next wave. Done.

---

## 4. What a card pack should feel like

1. Pack sits there. Big. Gem color on the pips.
2. Tap. Three real cards fan out. Big art. One line of text.
3. You pick. That card slams forward.
4. You plant it on a moon (if it is a gun) or it joins the sky (if it is a craft).
5. Fight the whole run. Next wave after next wave. No more cards.

If that loop is cool, the game is cool. If the cards stay tiny, nothing else will save it.

---

## 5. What we keep vs drop

**Keep**
- Guns on moons, crafts in the sky
- Lives + Home
- Credit
- 3-pick-1 draft
- Gem rarity
- The gun names (Lance, Halo, Well, Ion…)
- Bound main craft

**Drop from the fight**
- Mid-wave pauses for picks
- Tiny part cards with percents
- Extra bars on the combat screen (orbit, bond, forge)
- Ban, extra pack types, extra text on the card face

**Later, not now**
- Skill trees
- New enemies, new guns, new modes
- Fancy sound pass (fine, but after cards look right)

---

## 6. Build order

**1. Cards first.** Bigger. Art-first. One line of text. Pick screen is a card table, not a menu.

**3. Fast start.** A few packs. Plant. Fight the rest of the run with what you built.

**4. Then we play it** and see if the lane needs anything. Not before.

---

## 7. Card size target (so this is not vague)

On your phone (tall screen):

- A pick card should be about **as tall as half the screen** when it is the one you are looking at.
- Three-up: each card still tall enough that the gun art is a portrait, not an icon.
- Name readable from arm’s length.
- The Does / Hits / Extra block goes away if it crowds the art. One line under the name is enough.

Desktop: three cards in a row, each about a real poker-card on the desk. Not five tiny ones.

---

That is the plan. Simple TD. Simple draft. Cards that look like cards.
